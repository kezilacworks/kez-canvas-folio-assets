WHAT THIS MEANS FOR YOU

  The font travels with this template and with any site you build from it. You
  do not owe the authors anything and you do not need to credit them, though
  they would presumably not mind.

  What you may not do is sell the font by itself, or repackage it as a font
  product. Selling a website that happens to contain the font is not the same
  thing and is explicitly allowed.

  If you would rather not rely on a translated license, Paperlogy is a plain
  Korean sans-serif and swapping it out is a two-line change: replace the
  @font-face blocks at the top of styles.css and the preload link in
  index.html. Pretendard (SIL OFL 1.1, https://github.com/orioncactus/pretendard)
  is the closest drop-in.
